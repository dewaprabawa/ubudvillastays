Volumes below are directional tiers, not proprietary tool exports.

## Notes
- Niche terms like *private villa Ubud* often show low volume / high CPC — good for conversion pages.
- Broader *Bali itinerary* / beach keywords are high volume but weak fit; stay Ubud-culture-wellness focused.
- Peak booking interest historically mid-year and year-end; evergreen guides still capture research traffic year-round.
